The first thing to understand: **none of these is WhatsApp.** All three are
Business Solution Providers sitting on Meta's WhatsApp Business API. The
underlying messaging capability, the template approval process and Meta's
conversation charges are broadly the same whichever you pick.

You are choosing the software on top, and the support behind it.

## What is genuinely the same

- Template messages need Meta approval. Every provider waits in the same queue.
- Meta's conversation-based charges apply regardless.
- Green tick verification is Meta's decision, not the provider's.
- 24-hour customer service window rules are Meta's rules.

Any provider implying otherwise is selling you something that is not theirs to
sell.

## Where they differ

**Interakt** is the most Shopify-native of the three. Catalog sync, abandoned
cart and order notifications work with the least configuration. For a merchant
whose main need is transactional messaging tied to Shopify events, it is
generally the shortest path to working.

**AiSensy** leans towards campaigns — broadcast, segmentation, retargeting flows.
If your use case is marketing volume rather than transactional messaging, this is
where it is strongest. It also tends to be the more accessible option at the
lower end.

**Wati** is the most team-oriented. Shared inbox, agent assignment, routing. If
several people answer customer messages, this is the difference between a tool
and a chaos multiplier.

## Choosing by use case

| If your primary need is | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Order and shipping notifications from Shopify | Interakt |
| Broadcast campaigns and segmentation | AiSensy |
| A team answering customer conversations | Wati |
| A custom flow you will build yourself | Whichever has the best API docs |

## The things that actually bite

**Template rejection.** Meta rejects promotional templates routinely and the
reasons are frequently opaque. Ask any provider you are evaluating how they
support you through rejections, because you will need it.

**Opt-in hygiene.** Messaging people who did not opt in gets your number quality
rating downgraded, and a bad rating throttles your sending limits. This is the
most common way merchants damage a WhatsApp channel, and no provider protects
you from yourself.

**Number migration.** Moving a number between providers is possible but
disruptive. Treat the first choice as sticky for at least a year.

**API depth.** If you plan to build custom automations rather than use their
dashboard, evaluate the API before the UI. This is where the three diverge most
and where feature grids tell you least.

## My default

For a Shopify merchant who mainly wants order updates and abandoned cart
recovery working quickly: Interakt. For a brand running WhatsApp as a marketing
channel: AiSensy. For anyone with a support team: Wati.

Verify current pricing directly — it moves, and it is the one thing not worth
taking from a blog post.
